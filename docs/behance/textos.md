# Behance copy (English, short)

Sample data (reviews, return terms, sold-out items) is placeholder content for a university prototype.

## Project intro
We measured how much 25 D1 customers trust the online store (Schultz, 2006 model): 56.3/100 overall. Institutional trust sat at 61, interpersonal at 62, dispositional at 46. We redesigned a local copy to lift the first two and re-measure with the same 17-question survey.

## 1 · Secure payment
**Why:** data and payment security scored 3.36 / 5.
**What:** a simulated checkout with clear payment methods, security seals and three plain-language answers about how payments are protected.
**Target:** institutional trust, 61 → 71+.

## 2 · Returns and help, one click away
**Why:** policies and legal backing was the lowest institutional item (3.28 / 5), yet returns were the best rated (3.64) and hidden in the footer.
**What:** a three-line return summary on every product and in the cart, a floating Help button, and a short returns page.
**Target:** institutional 61 → 71+, interpersonal 62 → 70+.

## 3 · Honest "Sold out"
**Why:** integrity scored 3.56 / 5, and everything looked buyable.
**What:** out-of-stock products stay visible with a "Sold out" label and cannot be added to the cart.
**Target:** interpersonal trust, 62 → 70+.

## 4 · Reviews
**Why:** capability (3.52) and integrity (3.56) lean on what others say.
**What:** star ratings on cards and a reviews section on each product, clearly labeled as sample reviews.
**Target:** interpersonal trust, 62 → 70+.

## 5 · One consistent product sheet
**Why:** only one product had a description, in a different format from the rest.
**What:** seven fixed fields in the same order on every product; unknowns say "Not provided".
**Target:** capability perception (3.52 / 5), interpersonal 62 → 70+.

## 6 · Better search and A–Z order
**Why:** search only understood a handful of popular terms.
**What:** search across the whole catalog, alphabetical suggestions while typing, and A–Z / Z–A sorting.
**Target:** capability perception (3.52 / 5), interpersonal 62 → 70+.

## 7 · Your nearest store
**Why:** benevolence was the lowest interpersonal item (3.28 / 5); first-time buyers trust less (54) than repeat buyers (62).
**What:** pick your city and see the three nearest stores with address, hours and a support phone number.
**Target:** institutional 61 → 71+, interpersonal 62 → 70+.

## Hypotheses
H1 Institutional ≥ 71 · H2 Interpersonal ≥ 70 · H3 Dispositional stays within ±5 of 46.
